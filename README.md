# Bonefire Messaging 🔥

### Messaging, made personal.

A lightweight messaging platform designed to bring people
closer together through simple conversations, small communities,
and a more personal experience.

## Why Bonefire?

Messaging shouldn't feel complicated.

Most communication platforms have grown into complex ecosystems
filled with features you may never use. Bonefire takes a
different approach by focusing on what matters: connecting
with people and making the experience your own.

Whether you're catching up with friends, spending time in a
small group, or customizing your space, everything is designed
to feel simple, familiar, and enjoyable.

## What Makes Us Different?

- **Simple by Design**
  A clean, intuitive interface without unnecessary clutter.

- **Meaningful Connections**
  Private conversations and groups limited to 50 members,
  keeping communities smaller and more personal.

- **Made for You**
  Express yourself through custom profiles, moods, statuses,
  themes, and more.

- **Discover & Customize**
  Explore free and paid themes, emoji packs, stickers, and
  profile decorations created by the community.

- **Privacy Matters**
  Control who can contact you, manage your conversations,
  and personalize your privacy preferences.

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Chats | Private messaging with friends. |
| Groups | Personal communities of up to 50 members. |
| Discover | Free and paid customization packs. |
| Profiles | Avatars, banners, moods, and statuses. |
| Settings | Privacy, appearance, and chat preferences. |

## Our Vision

We believe messaging should be more than exchanging messages.

It should be a space that feels like yours, where conversations
come naturally, communities feel welcoming, and personalization
is part of the experience.

No unnecessary complexity. Just you, your friends, and a
messaging experience that feels personal.
